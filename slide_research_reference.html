Papers / Sources Referred for Developing Solution

1) GraphCast – Learning Skillful Medium-Range Global Weather Forecasting
Lam et al., Science, 2023 (Google DeepMind)
Used for: Primary AI constituent model input for synoptic steering & medium-range trajectory modelling on spherical multiscale graph mesh (<60s latency).
2) Data-Driven Weather Ensembling & Meta-Model Stacking
Weyn et al., arXiv:2403.15598, 2024 (Microsoft Weather AI)
Used for: Core scientific proof that multi-model ML stacking beats raw physical NWPs by 4%–17% RMSE, replacing blind ensemble averaging with dynamic weights.
3) PoET – Post-processing of Ensembles with Transformers
Dramsch et al. / ECMWF Lab, arXiv:2303.17195, 2023–2024
Used for: Methodological anchor for ChronoShift Layer — cross-attention dynamically shifting trust from short-range AI (D1–2) to physical NWPs at medium range (D5–10).
4) Physics-Informed ML & Asymmetric Extreme Quantile Loss
Reichstein et al., Nature, 2019; Gagne et al., JAMES, 2020; Koenker, 1978
Used for: Formulating PeakGuard Loss (pinball loss τ=0.98 penalizing under-prediction 10× more to retain >65mm flood peaks) and ThermoCheck Gate.
5) NCMRWF Operational Forecasts & IMD High-Resolution Observations
Rajagopal et al., 2020; Pai et al., Mausam, 2014; Srivastava et al., 2009
Used for: Operational 12 km dynamical input (NCUM), 23-member uncertainty spread (NEPS-G), and IMD 0.25° gridded ground-truth verification archives.
